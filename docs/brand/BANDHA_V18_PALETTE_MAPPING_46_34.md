## Spatial design translation
- v1.8 uses large dark technical hero/scene areas juxtaposed with lighter editorial/consulting sections.
- AIM applies deep surfaces to navigation and topbar (technical identity), light paper to dense evidence/notes work areas, and restrained copper accents. It does **not** imitate cinematic website sections in operational tables.
- COR source, INS source, engineer-authored draft, and status remain text-labeled in addition to colored edges. No authority or engineering calculation changes.
- This phase replaces the earlier speculative Phase 46.33 color values by a final cascade override. Earlier CSS declarations remain for backward source-contract compatibility until a full mounted app visual cleanup.
- This phase is **color/material alignment**, not a copy of v1.8 fonts, typography, content, or imagery, and not a claim of pixel-identical website appearance.
